Privacy Policy
Last Updated: August 9, 2026

1. Overview
Stelr ("we," "our," or "the extension") is an open-source, companion browser extension designed to synchronize browser bookmarks with a user's self-hosted Stelr web application instance. We are deeply committed to user privacy and data ownership. 

2. No Data Collection or Centralized Storage
We do not collect, harvest, track, store, or monetize any personal information, browsing history, or user data. There are no central databases or analytics trackers bundled with this extension. 

3. Data Transmission and User Control
The extension requires access to your browser's local bookmarks and storage to function. This data is handled in the following manner:
- Direct Transmission: All synchronization of bookmarks occurs directly between your local browser environment and your privately configured, self-hosted Stelr host URL.
- No Third-Party Access: Your data is never transmitted to the developer, Google, or any third-party services. You retain 100% ownership and physical control over your link infrastructure.
- Local Storage: Your authentication tokens and server configuration configurations are stored securely inside your local browser profile using standard extension storage APIs.

4. Permission Usage
- 'bookmarks': Used solely to read and monitor your local folder structures to sync them with your personal database.
- 'storage': Used strictly to save your custom host URL and local sync preferences.
- 'activeTab': Used to let you quickly save the current webpage title and URL to your instance.
- 'alarms': Used to trigger periodic, automated sync loops.

5. Changes to This Policy
Because this extension handles data locally and directly to your own infrastructure, this policy will only change if new local browser features are introduced.

6. Contact
For questions regarding this policy or the open-source codebase, please contact the developer via the official repository or the developer support email listed on the Chrome Web Store.
